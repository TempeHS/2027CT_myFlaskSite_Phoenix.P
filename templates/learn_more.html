Spain's recipe for success

Spain’s men have underscored their status as the 21st century’s standout national team by adding two FIFA World Cup™ stars to their crest. The classes of 2010 and 2026 may differ somewhat, not least because football itself has changed, but there is an unmistakeable thread linking the two – a shared understanding of the game.

Rudi Garcia steps down

Garcia leaves Belgium

Rudi Garcia's time with the Red Devils is coming to an end after guiding Belgium to the quarter-finals of the FIFA World Cup 2026™, where they were defeated by eventual champions Spain.

The Belgian Football Association confirmed today that Garcia’s contract will not be extended. Garcia thanked his players, staff, and fans for their support throughout the campaign.

Spain crowned champions

La Roja are on top of the world for the second time in their history following a dominant tournament in which they conceded only one goal en route to victory over Argentina in the final.

After a slow start, Spain surged through the knockout rounds with commanding wins over Uruguay, Austria, Portugal, Belgium, and France before lifting the trophy.
