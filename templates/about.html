About Scores Squad

Welcome to Scores Squad, your number one source for World Cup coverage.

Scores squad is a website for the Tempe High School soccer club. This is a website that will provide all the soccer fans in the school information about the latest matches on the main page. You can also search up past matches and rewatch the legends play for your entertainment; our page also has another section which ensures the user to learn new soccer tricks or tips or drills from video guides to build up your skill. The tricks and drills are in sections from beginner, know a bit about soccer or pro as this can keep track of your level to make sure you can perform the drill or trick.

Our squad see people spending too much time on studying for hours not giving their brains a break which cause them to lose interest in their hobbies but our squad allows the soccer fans around the school which wants to watch or learn new tricks in their free time, give their brain a break and the best thing is our club ensures that you and your friends can get the chance to perform your or their dream tricks. It's a good way to get teens and kids active. It also doesn't have any accounts to use as it's a free safety website with no ads and no spams about the latest news.

By creating this website with all these soccer tips and videos we can engage the students at Tempe High school to take a break and stay active which is a key factor for everyone to stay fit and healthy. They can develop a healthy routine, learn new skills, copy training drills, watch their favourite team play at home or at school during free time and become a pro at soccer.

Score squads focus on teens and kids in Tempe High School or other schools with ages ranging from 5 - 18 who are interested in soccer or want to have a healthy routine and learn new tricks. These kids don't need to sign up, create an account or ask a parent or guardian, just search up our website and start the drills or matches. The people who want to use this website must need a good navigation controls which our website has with a bar at the top with the main topics and you can search up for matches, drills and tricks on the side.

Score Squad connects to our students to help, engage and watch the latest news and tricks as it makes it easier for students to get involved and stay active. With clear information and navigation, it allows the students to easily navigate through our website which we expect for no misleading information or false news and students' daily routine and skill improves. This outcome is only achievable with our great programmers and designers as it took time to plan carefully to make every inch, hidden corner of this website fully functional for our audience to use.
